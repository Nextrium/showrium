The SPDX identifier for the app is `AGPL-3.0-only`. For the SDK it is `Apache-2.0`.

## Option 1: the AGPL-3.0 (free)

You may use, study, change and share Showrium, including for commercial purposes, under the terms of the [GNU Affero General Public License, version 3](LICENSE). In short:

- If you **share** Showrium or a changed version, you must share its source code under the AGPL-3.0.
- If you **run a changed version as a service** that other people use over a network (a website, an API), you must offer those users the source code of your version, under the AGPL-3.0 (section 13).
- You must keep the copyright and licence notices.

This summary is for convenience. The licence text is what applies.

## Option 2: a commercial licence (paid)

If you can't or don't want to meet the AGPL-3.0's terms, Nextrium offers a commercial licence. For example, you may want to:

- run a changed version as a service without publishing your changes
- embed Showrium in a product you distribute without sharing its source

Write to **legal@showrium.com** with your company and how you plan to use Showrium.

Nextrium can offer this because every outside contribution comes with a [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) that allows it.

## The SDK (Apache-2.0)

`packages/sdk-ts` is the client you put in your own app to call the Showrium API. It's under the permissive Apache-2.0 licence, so you can use it in open or closed software without the AGPL's obligations. Using the hosted Showrium API through the SDK doesn't place your app under the AGPL.
