# Changelog

## v0.1.0: private beta (unreleased)

The first release of Showrium to app.showrium.com, for invited testers.

### For people
- **Write once, post everywhere:** one idea, link, photo, document or voice note becomes a post for each platform you use, in your voice. Nothing posts without your approval.
- **Sources that work for everyone:** public GitHub repositories, blogs and websites (with or without a feed), YouTube channels, podcasts, photos and documents (iPhone photos included), and a daily question matched to your work.
- **Ideas and Posts:** post-worthy moments appear as ideas. Posts show where they came from. Both pages use a list with a detail panel.
- **Publishing:** post directly to Bluesky, Mastodon, LinkedIn and X, or tap-to-post anywhere else. Schedule posts, or approve a batch in one tap.
- **Autopilot levels,** from ideas only up to full autopilot (limited by plan).
- **Insights:** likes, replies and themes from your audience, where platforms allow it.
- **Video (early):** faceless explainer videos with captions.
- **Teams and billing:** workspaces, roles, invites, an audit log, plans with Paystack and Lemon Squeezy, and credits.
- **For developers:** a REST API with docs, a TypeScript SDK and an MCP server.
- **Accounts:**
  - sign in with Google, GitHub or email
  - email confirmation and password reset
  - a guided welcome for new people
- **Design:** a responsive redesign with light and dark themes.

### Access and safety
- Invite-only: sign-up needs a single-use invite link, and showrium.com only collects the waitlist.
- House style: no hashtags, no em dashes, and numbers or handles that aren't in your source are flagged.
- Rate limits, plan limits, and a switch that pauses publishing in an emergency.
