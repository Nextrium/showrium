# Showrium Contributor License Agreement (version 1)

Thank you for contributing to Showrium. This agreement explains the rights you give Nextrium in your contributions. You keep ownership of your work.

It lets Nextrium:
- keep Showrium under the AGPL-3.0 (see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md))
- also offer it under a commercial licence, which funds the project

In return, Nextrium promises to keep your contributions available as open source (section 6).

**How to sign:** when you open your first pull request, a bot asks you to sign. Reply on the pull request with:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

That records your agreement, linked to your GitHub account, for this and all your future contributions. If you're contributing for a company, see section 9.

If you contribute as part of your job, your employer may own your work. In that case, a person who can sign for the company should email **legal@showrium.com**. Include:

- the GitHub accounts allowed to contribute for it

The company then agrees to this agreement for the Contributions those people make. Each of them still signs on their first pull request.
