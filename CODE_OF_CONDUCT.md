# Code of conduct

Showrium is built in the open by people from many countries, backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone taking part (in issues, pull requests, reviews, the contributors portal and any Nextrium community space) agrees to this code.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person. Explain the reason behind a request.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Keep private information private: other people's data, accounts, keys and anything shared in confidence.

## What isn't accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background, body, ability or beliefs.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or community spaces.
- Using Showrium or its community to spam, mislead or break the rules of the platforms it posts to.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to **legal@showrium.com**. Include what happened, where, when, and links or screenshots if you have them. Reports are read only by the people who handle them, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.

You'll get a reply within 3 working days. If a report involves someone who handles reports, they take no part in it.

## What happens

Depending on what happened, the response can be:

1. **A private note** explaining what was wrong.
2. **A warning**, with clear consequences if it continues.
3. **A temporary ban** from the project's spaces.
4. **A permanent ban** for serious or repeated harm.

Decisions are explained to the person concerned, who can ask for one review.

## Where it applies

In every Showrium and Nextrium project space, and outside them when someone represents the project.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
